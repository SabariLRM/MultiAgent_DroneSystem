How to read this replay
Blue drone: flying to a warehouse (hub) to collect a parcel, or loading it
Orange drone: carrying a parcel to a customer (dark box below it = parcel, diamond = express)
Green drone: going to a swap station for a fresh battery, or being swapped
Grey drone: free, parked at a hub or flying back to one
Glowing ring around a drone is its battery: green above 50 %, amber 25–50 %, red below 25 %. Red and green lights are its navigation lights; a box under it is a parcel (red = express), lowered to the customer on a winch
Line: the route the drone has booked, climbs and descents included. Routes are booked in space and time, so lines can cross without the drones ever meeting
True scale: buildings and flight heights are to scale (layers at 30, 60, 90 m; each planning block of up to 45, 75 or 105 m is drawn as one to four towers no taller than the block). A drone can fly over a block on any layer above it. Drones are drawn larger than life so you can see them, more so when the camera is far away; their shadows show where they are
Red box: a no-fly zone and the layers it closes (dashed outline = announced, not active yet). Rings on the ground are customers waiting for an order
Trail: where the drone actually flew in the last 30 seconds, in 3D: climbs, smoothed corners, gusts and swerves. An amber ring means collision avoidance (ORCA) is steering it around another drone right now
Bubble (optional): the separation minimum around a drone, 40 m sideways and 15 m up and down. It turns red, and a red line joins the two drones, when another drone is inside it
Arrow (optional): where the drone will be in 8 seconds at its current velocity. The compass at the top left shows the wind

Drag to orbit, right-drag or two fingers to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom down to street level. Click a drone to follow it: the camera chases it, and Drone view rides along behind it. The light theme is daytime, the dark theme dusk.
