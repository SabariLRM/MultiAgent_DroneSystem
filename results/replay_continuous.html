Drone fleet replay

How to read this replay
Blue drone: flying to a warehouse (hub) to collect a parcel, or loading it
Orange drone: carrying a parcel to a customer. The box under it is the parcel (brown = standard, red = express)
Green drone: going to a swap station for a fresh battery, or being swapped
Grey drone: free, parked at a hub or flying back to one
Ring around a drone is its battery: green above 50 %, amber 25–50 %, red below 25 %
Dashed line: the route the drone has booked. Routes are booked in space and time, so two routes can cross without the drones ever meeting
Ring with a number: a customer waiting for that order. Grey = no drone yet, blue = a drone is collecting it, orange = on its way
Customers live in buildings. A building with a white target takes the parcel on its roof: a 30 m roof is served from 60 m, a 60 m roof from 90 m. A small house gets it in its garden, lowered from 30 m. Darker buildings are taller
H = warehouse (hub) where parcels are loaded. Lightning = battery-swap station. Red hatched area = temporary no-fly zone
Altitude: L2 in a label is the drone's flight layer (1 = lowest, about 30 m each). Darker buildings are taller; a drone can fly over a building on any layer above its height. Drones stacked over the same spot are drawn side by side
Trail: where the drone actually flew in the last 30 seconds, so you can see smoothed corners, gusts and swerves, not cell-by-cell hops
Amber dashed ring: collision avoidance (ORCA) is steering this drone around another one right now
Red line between two drones: a loss of separation, closer than 40 m horizontally and 15 m vertically at the same time. Drones fly continuously here: labels show their height in metres
Wind (the tile above the map): where the wind blows on the map and how fast, at the centre of the city

Click any drone, on the map or in the fleet list, to follow it and see what it is doing and why.
